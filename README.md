## ✨ Features That Help You

PolyPulse isn't just one tool—it's a whole toolbox designed for game asset creation. Here are the main things it can do for you.

### 🛠️ Automatic Mesh Repair
Got a messy 3D model that has holes, broken edges, or double faces? PolyPulse scans your entire mesh for these common issues. Then, with a single click, it zips them up and fixes the geometry. This is perfect for repairing problems from photogrammetry or scans.

### 📦 LOD Generation
In video games, distant objects don't need as much detail. LOD (Level of Detail) means having a simple, lightweight version of your model when the camera is far away. PolyPulse can automatically create these low-poly and medium-poly versions for you. You instantly get several versions of your model that seamlessly swap in the game engine to enhance performance.

### 🗺️ UV Atlas Creator
UV mapping is how a 2D texture wraps around your 3D model. It's a tricky job. PolyPulse can auto-unwrap your model and pack the parts into a tidy "UV Atlas." This makes texturing easier and makes your game's texture memory use simpler. No more manual unwrapping required.

### 🚧 Collider Generator
Colliders are invisible shapes games use for physics. They make floors solid, characters collide, and gameplay work. PolyPulse creates the right, clean colliders at the push of a button. Run convex hull, simple, or box collider types, perfect for any game.

### 🚀 Engine Export

Forget exporting formats and hoping for perfection. PolyPulse prepares your models for direct use in the big three engines:

*   **Unreal Engine 5:** Export meshes, colliders, and LODs in the expected format.
*   **Unity:** Get a clean, ready-to-import package.
*   **Godot:** Bring everything usable in your Godot project.

In essence, PolyPulse automates everything technical so that you can focus on the creative part of 3D art.
